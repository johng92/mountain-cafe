Our Story

Made for mountain mornings.

A small café built around good coffee, honest food, and a love for the hills.

From a small idea to a favorite corner

Mountain Café began with a simple dream: create a welcoming place where locals and travelers could share a warm drink and enjoy the beauty around them.

Today, we still keep things simple. We source quality ingredients, prepare our food with care, and treat every guest like a friend dropping by for a visit.

“The best conversations often begin with a cup of coffee.”

Come as you are

Whether you're starting a hike, meeting an old friend, reading a book, or simply looking for a quiet place to breathe, there's a seat waiting for you.
